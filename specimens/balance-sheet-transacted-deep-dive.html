Transacted — a deeper dive

Nothing landed for this band because its data isn't a part-of-whole (that's the donut) or a peer comparison (that's the order book). Its three numbers are bound by one identity: volume × price = value. The average is volume-weighted — a fact no option has shown yet.

The 140 ac-ft is two real deals: TX-3 (60 ac-ft @ $90) and TX-4 (80 ac-ft @ $120). That's the raw material these charts work with. Three analytical stories are worth telling:

Value as area

If width is volume and height is price, the area is the money. One picture holds all three numbers.

Price dispersion

The deals cleared at $90 and $120. Where does the $107.14 average actually sit, and why?

Weighting

The average leans toward $120 because that deal was larger. The weighting should be visible, not implied.
