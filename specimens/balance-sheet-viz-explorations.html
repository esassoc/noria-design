Balance Flow — three idioms per band, no repeated meter

Three horizontal bars in a row read as one gesture repeated. So only the order book keeps the bar — it earns it, being a true two-quantity comparison. The other two bands switch idiom entirely.

The band data even asks for it: Transacted is money arithmetic, not a part-of-whole — it wants a ledger, not a chart. Allocation genuinely is a part-of-whole — it wants a real chart, just a radial or unit one, not another bar. The result is a deliberate rhythm: chart → ledger → chart.
