Transacted — the value block

No average line. And the band must always carry the three figures we compute: Volume Transacted, Transaction Price (total), and Avg Price per ac-ft.

Those three aren't independent — they're the two sides and the area of one rectangle: Volume × Avg Price = Transaction Price. So the average price needs no line of its own; it simply is the block's height. Three refinements of that one idea below.

What it shows

The block is measured like a drawing: its height is the average price, its width is the volume, and the area they enclose is the transaction total.

Why it fits

Every figure has one job and one place. The average reads as a dimension, not an extra annotation — so the line disappears but the number stays.
